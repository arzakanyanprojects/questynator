# Starter valley guides (draft routes)

Quest-only guides for the six starter valleys, levels 1-6. Every step is accept, complete or turn in.
No tips, trainer visits or shortcuts. When the valley is done the Route Planner takes over.

How each route was made: the quest set, levels, prerequisites and places come from our own
`Data/QuestData.lua`. The order groups the work by area. It was then checked against RestedXP's
Forever guides (reference only, nothing copied: their guides are CC BY-NC-SA).

Class tags in brackets, like [Warlock], mean only that class does the step.

## Data notes

- Minor Manifestation of Earth (n5891) has one spawn in our QuestData, in Durotar. The guide data places the Dwarf
  spirit (94374/94375) at Dun Morogh 24.9, 61.9 and the Tauren one (1520/1521) at Mulgore 53.7, 80.2 with `at`
  overrides. Check both in game.
- The Goddess Provides (97979, Night Elf, any class) and A Light in the Darkness (98389, Undead, any class) have no
  objectives in our data. Check in game what they ask for.
